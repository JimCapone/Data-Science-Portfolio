# Superstore Profitability & Risk Analysis

Conducted end-to-end profitability analysis on retail transaction data to evaluate structural margin performance and risk concentration across business divisions.
Developed transaction-level profit margin diagnostics and negative margin rate metrics to distinguish sustainable growth drivers from loss-generating segments.

Performed time-series forecasting using rolling-average modeling with out-of-sample validation to project 6-month forward profit trends. Consolidated findings into
an executive-ready Tableau dashboard featuring interactive division filtering and a forward projection visualization. <br>
<br/>

## Skills Demonstrated:
Profitability Analysis | Margin diagnostics | Time-Series Forecasting | Dashboard Design | Business Storytelling
